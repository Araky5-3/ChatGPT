1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Perceptron Network presents itself as an AI/Web3 infrastructure project combining decentralized bandwidth and data collection with an incentive and reputation layer for AI agents. Public materials claim 700,000+ nodes globally and describe PERCs as tiered micro-NFTs that reward contributions and establish trust signals, alongside a separate $PERC fungible token used for community rewards and agent staking.

There is evidence of a real project and ecosystem activity: public documentation, a Chrome extension with hundreds of reviews, a reported strategic financing round, and a 2025 merger involving BlockMesh. However, the token currently presents material exchange-listing risks. Public market data is fragmented across multiple tokens using the PERC name/symbol, identifiable Solana liquidity is extremely thin, Phantom labels the Solana PERC token unverified, and the top 10 holders reportedly control 98.28% of that token’s supply.

✅ Positive Finding: Credible Product Surface

Public documentation, an active website, a Chrome Web Store listing, a reported BlockMesh merger, and claimed node/user activity indicate more than a ticker-only launch and support continued product diligence.

⛔ Critical Finding: Canonical Token Not Established

Project documentation and currently discoverable market contracts do not provide enough clarity to establish a single, authoritative, exchange-ready PERC contract and token distribution model. An exchange should not rely on ticker or name matching alone.

⚠️ Overall Risk Rating: HIGH / RED

Listing recommendation at this stage is do not list. Place PERC on enhanced due-diligence / watchlist status until contract identity, liquidity, holder distribution, and quantitative tokenomics can be independently verified. Principal blockers are an unconfirmed canonical contract, multiple public PERC tokens, ~$2.5K–$2.8K observable liquidity, 98.28% top-10 concentration, and insufficient exchange-grade tokenomics disclosure.
2. PROJECT & PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Perceptron Network describes itself as an AI-agent infrastructure and decentralized data network. Its stated model involves users sharing unused bandwidth, collecting and providing data for AI applications, rewarding participants, and allowing AI agents to use PERCs as trust and engagement signals. Agents are described as generating revenue through PERC sales or auctions, while $PERC is distributed to qualifying community members and used for agent staking.

2.1 Protocol Functions
Decentralized bandwidth sharing: Users contribute unused bandwidth to the data network
AI data collection: Nodes gather or provide data used by AI applications
PERC micro-NFTs: Tiered on-chain trust metrics rewarding contribution and engagement
Agent incentive layer: AI agents use PERCs as trust, reputation, and engagement signals
$PERC utility token: Community rewards, ecosystem access, and agent staking
2.2 Vision & Impact

A 2025 announcement following the Perceptron / BlockMesh merger reported 700,000+ active nodes and 200,000+ users engaged through Perceptron Agents. A third-party database records a July 2026 strategic round and approximately $6.5M in funding; aggregator figures are inconsistent and should not be treated as verified without primary-source confirmation.
